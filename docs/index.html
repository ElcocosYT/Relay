RELAYfor Windows

Play your own music or search eight online sources. Use sounds in calls and games, send everything through your mic, and show what you're playing on Discord.

Download for Windows Take the tour

Latest version · Windows 10 and 11 · 125 MB · Free, no ads

  1. Add your music folders and Relay reads the tags, covers and lyrics in your files. Sort and organise everything the way you like.

    • Choose, move and resize the columns
    • Give any song a colour tag or a hotkey
    • Different versions of a song share one row
    • Drag in songs and folders, or import playlists

  2. All your albums as covers. Point at one and its record slides out. The album that is playing keeps its record turning.

    • Pin your favourite albums so they stay first
    • Play an album or open its menu from the cover
    • Show only albums, singles or one artist

  3. Open an album to see its songs, its year and how long it is. The song that is playing is marked.

    • Click the name, year or artist to change them
    • Drag songs to change their order
    • Albums with more than one disc show each disc in its own column
    • Click an artist's name to open their page

  4. Everyone in your library, each with their picture. A song with more than one artist counts for each of them.

    • Set your own picture for any artist
    • Sort by name, albums or songs
    • Add artists to your favourites

  5. An artist's page shows their latest release, their most played songs, their albums and singles, the albums they appear on, and all their songs.

    • Put your own image, GIF or video at the top
    • The album that is playing shows in your theme colour
    • Click an album or an artist's name to go there

  6. The song that is playing is in the middle, with the songs before and after it at the sides. The lyrics move with the song, and the queue is on the right.

    • Lyrics word by word from yedits and lrc.red, or line by line from LRCLIB
    • No lyrics found? Relay can make timed lyrics on your PC with Whisper
    • Click a line to go to that part of the song
    • Drag songs in the queue to change the order

  7. One click hides the rest of Relay. The cover and the controls are on the left, the lyrics are in the middle, and the next songs are on the right. The colours come from the cover.

  8. Put songs and sound clips on pads and play them with a click or a hotkey. They play on top of your music, several at once, and go through your mic together with your voice.

    • Drag songs from your library onto a pad
    • Play the whole song or only part of it, cut on its waveform
    • A hotkey for every pad, plus loop and Stop all

  9. Search YouTube, Deezer, SoundCloud and five more sources at the same time. Play a result right away, or add it to your library with its cover.

    • The home page shows your recent searches, what you played and what's popular
    • Find albums, playlists and artists, not only songs
    • Turn sources on or off, or change their order

  10. Relay adds a virtual microphone. Choose it in Discord or in a game, and people hear your music and sounds together with your voice.

    • Separate volume for music, mic, sounds and the total
    • The music gets quieter while you talk
    • An equalizer with presets

Online

Eight sources, one search

Relay searches all of these at the same time. You don't need an account for any of them.

YouTubeSongs, mixes and live sets
DeezerFull albums from record labels
SoundCloudRemixes and independent releases
AudiusMusic from independent artists
yeditsEdits and reworks by fans
wavernrsYe comps, edits and an archive
unreleased.worldUnreleased songs, demos and snippets
Internet ArchiveOld recordings and live shows

You can also copy a link to a song, album or playlist from Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud, and Relay offers to add it when you come back.

Listening parties

Listen together

Start a party and your friends hear the same song at the same time, with its cover and lyrics. If someone doesn't have a song, Relay sends it to them.

Guests and editorsGuests listen. Editors can also add songs and fix the lyrics.
ChatSend messages, with Discord emoji, during the party.
Kick and banRemove someone, or stop them from coming back to that party.
Join from DiscordWhile you host, your Discord status has a Join button.
Share

Relay Recap

Your top songs, artists and listening time as an image you can post on Discord, X or Instagram. You can also share one song as a card.

Share songs as a linkSongs you found online can be shared as a Relay link. When a friend opens it, the song is added to their Relay library, ready to play.
Albums and playlists tooIn Online, share a whole album or playlist the same way, or pick several songs and send them as one link.
Themes

Pick your colour

Choose a colour and all of Relay changes to match: the sidebar, the buttons, the glows and the waves. Try it here with the picker or the colours below, and this page changes too.

  • Ready-made colours, or any colour you want
  • Separate colours for the bars, the equalizer and the waves
  • Save your own themes
  • Turn each effect on or off
