Take the tour

Everything you play, in one app

Library

Your files, your way

Point Relay at your music folders and it reads everything in them: tags, covers and lyrics. Sort it how you like and keep it tidy.

  • Pick, reorder and resize the columns
  • Colour tags and a hotkey for any song
  • Versions of the same song grouped in one row
  • Drag songs and folders straight in, or import playlists
Albums

Covers first

Big covers and nothing in the way. Hover one and its record slides out, painted in the colours of the cover. The album playing now keeps its record spinning.

  • Pin your favourites so they're always first
  • Play it or open its menu right on the cover
  • Filter by album, single or artist as you type
Artists

Everyone you listen to

Every artist with their own glow, taken from their picture. Open one and you get their albums, their singles, the albums they appear in and every song.

  • Collaborations counted for everyone on the track
  • Set your own picture for any artist
  • Play everything by them in one click
Now Playing · Lyrics

Lyrics that sing along

Synced lyrics that move with the song, line by line, the way you'd expect from a streaming app. Click a line to jump there.

  • The playing line fills word by word
  • Found for you, or written and timed by hand
  • Precise timing with Whisper, right on your PC

Now Playing · Queue

Your queue, cover by cover

Now Playing shows what's coming as a cover flow. Hover a cover to light it up, click it to jump there, drag songs to reorder.

  • Each cover glows with its own colours
  • Versions of the same song stay together
  • Picks up where you left off when you open Relay

Soundboard

Sounds on cue, over your music

Put songs and sounds on pads and fire them in a call or a game. They play over whatever's on, several at once, and go out through your mic along with your voice. Click a pad to try it.

  • Drag songs from your library straight onto a pad
  • Play the whole song or just a clip, cut on its real waveform
  • A hotkey for every pad, loop, and Stop all
Online

Search seven services at once

Type once and every service answers. Play a song straight away, or import it into your library with its cover, ready to play offline.

  • A home page with your recent searches, what you played and what's trending
  • Albums, playlists and artists, not just songs
  • Put a service first, or switch it off

Copy a link, import it

Copy a song, album or playlist from Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud, and Relay offers to bring it in when you come back.

Whole playlists at once

Paste a playlist or album link and every song comes in, with its cover, ready to play offline.

Full albums, not previews

Label albums from Deezer play complete, and SoundCloud albums that only have 30-second previews say so up front.

Mixer

Your music, through your mic

Relay sets up a virtual microphone. Pick it in Discord or a game and your friends hear your music and your sounds, mixed with your own voice.

  • Music, mic, sounds and master, each on its own fader
  • Ducking: the music dips while you talk
  • An equalizer, and each channel to the device you pick
Listening parties

Listen together, in sync

Start a party and your friends hear exactly what you play, at the same moment, with your names, covers and lyrics. Songs they don't have are sent to them directly.

Guests and editorsGuests listen along. Editors can add songs and fix the lyrics live.
Live chatWith Discord emoji. Messages pop up over the party button.
Kick and banRemove someone, or keep them out of that party for good.
Join from DiscordYour status shows a Join button while you host.
Share

Your Relay Recap

Your top songs, artists and minutes as an image, ready to paste into Discord, X or Instagram. Or share any song as a card of its own.

Themes

Any colour you like

Pick a colour and all of Relay follows: the sidebar, the glows, the buttons and the waves. Try it here: this whole page changes with it.

  • Ready-made themes or any colour you pick
  • Save your own looks
  • Turn each effect on or off
